# geohazards: An R package for extracting global climate risk, susceptibility, and vulnerability data

Downloads and reads global datasets related to climate risk,
susceptibility and vulnerability directly into R, without manual
downloading. Includes the GLO-30 HAND (Height Above the Nearest
Drainage) raster for flood susceptibility, and the geological risk
cartography published by the Geological Survey of Brazil (SGB/CPRM)
through its GeoSGB and RIGeo services.
