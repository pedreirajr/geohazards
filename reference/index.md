# Package index

## Flood susceptibility

GLO-30 HAND (Height Above the Nearest Drainage), read remotely from
Cloud Optimized GeoTIFFs.

- [`read_hand()`](https://pedreirajr.github.io/geohazards/reference/read_hand.md)
  : Read the GLO-30 HAND raster for a geographic area

## Geological risk cartography (SGB/CPRM)

Risk sectorisation, flood mapping and disaster occurrences published by
the Geological Survey of Brazil, plus its technical reports.

- [`read_sgb()`](https://pedreirajr.github.io/geohazards/reference/read_sgb.md)
  : Read a geological risk product from the SGB
- [`read_sgb_report()`](https://pedreirajr.github.io/geohazards/reference/read_sgb_report.md)
  : Read a technical report from the SGB repository (RIGeo)

## Catalogue and lookup

What exists before downloading it, and how to name the area you want.

- [`sgb_products()`](https://pedreirajr.github.io/geohazards/reference/sgb_products.md)
  : Catalogue of SGB risk products
- [`sgb_inventory()`](https://pedreirajr.github.io/geohazards/reference/sgb_inventory.md)
  : Inventory of SGB products
- [`sgb_municipalities()`](https://pedreirajr.github.io/geohazards/reference/sgb_municipalities.md)
  : List Brazilian municipalities known to the SGB
